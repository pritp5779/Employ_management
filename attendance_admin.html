Day timing

An attendance day runs from its start time until the next day's start time — a punch out after midnight still belongs to the day the shift started. This is the same rule Orders and Targets use. Default is 10:00 AM every day.

Shoes

Shoes photo taken at every Punch In and checked by the AI: no shoes / slippers / crocs / sandals = Punch In blocked; proper shoes get a cleanliness rating 1–5. Click a photo to open it.

Refused punches

Punch In / Out tries that were refused because the employee was outside their office area (or had no office / no GPS). Nothing was saved for these.

Punch-in reminders

An employee who is inside an office area when the punch-in time has passed and who has not punched in gets a pop-up on whatever HRMS page is open on their phone. If HRMS is closed, the same reminder reaches the phone as a notification (the employee taps "Allow notifications" once on the punch page). Repeats until the limit; the list here shows who is still not in. Stops the moment they punch in. Nothing is sent by WhatsApp or SMS, no keys involved.
iPhone gets the closed-app notification only when HRMS was added to the Home Screen.
