Import from Excel

Upload your Excel file. Every row is matched to a shop that is already in the app by its mobile number (the shop name is only used when a row has no number). Matched shops get the pending amount from your file, shops that are not in the app yet are added. You see everything before anything is saved.

Drop your Excel file here or click to choose
.xlsx, .xls or .csv — a list with Shop name, Mobile and Pending amount is enough
What the file can contain

Shops / pending list — Shop name, Mobile, Pending amount (and, if you have them, Area, Owner, Address, Note, As on date). Matched shops keep their own name; blank Owner / Address / Mobile in the app are filled from your file. A minus amount means the shop paid in advance.

Orders (old bills) — Shop name or Mobile, Date, Bill no, Product, Size, Qty, Rate (or only Amount), Discount, Payment, Paid amount, Due date. Rows with the same Bill no make one bill.

Payments — Shop name or Mobile, Date, Amount, Mode (Cash / UPI / Bank / Cheque / Credit note), Reference, Bill no. A payment clears the oldest bills first, or the bill you name.

Ledger — Shop name or Mobile, Date, Debit, Credit, Narration: debit lines become bills, credit lines become payments, a line “Opening balance” becomes the shop's pending amount.

Dates as dd/mm/yyyy · ₹, commas and “5000/-” are fine · each sheet is recognised by its headings and you can change the columns before importing · uploading the same file again is safe, what is already in the app is skipped.
