Automatic penalties

For every employee with fixed working hours — not one person. Sales employees have no fixed hours and are never charged automatically; their penalties are written by hand and come off commission.

The check runs by itself every minute, from the same working time and late-alert grace you set on the Attendance page. Required hours are the length of each employee's working time (start → end), counted from their punch-in to punch-out — set a working-time end on Attendance for the hours rules to work. Days of leave, WFH, holiday, week-off and Sundays are skipped, and a rule only charges days from the moment you switched it on.
